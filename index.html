Why this guidebook

Poland's deep-tech ecosystem is entering a new stage

Poland has spent the last decade building a substantial deep-tech base. Today, 329 companies operate across nine technology sectors, supported by a large scientific and engineering talent pool, billions of euros in public R&D funding and a rapidly growing venture ecosystem.

This guidebook maps the market from an investor's perspective: where Poland is already producing global leaders, where international capital is entering, and where strong technological foundations remain underexploited.
